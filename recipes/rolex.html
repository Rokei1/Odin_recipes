Rolex

Rolex is a Ugandan street dish made of chapati and eggs with tomatoes and raw cabbage and at times may add beef or chicken. ie: Not to be mistaken with rolex watch

Ingredients

  • Chapati dough
  • eggs
  • Tomatoes
  • Sliced cabbages
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt
  • Onions

Steps

  1. Rollout the dough: Rollout the dough to good round dough
  2. Heat the pan to a 150c
  3. Pour 3 table spoons of cooking oil on the pan and let it hit for two minutes
  4. Fry the chapati dough to a golden brown color on both sides (and may become crisp ) . Repeat the process for the chapatis
  5. Add 3 table spoons of cooking oil the pan
  6. Break 2-4 eggs in a container, add salt and chopped onions (May also add sliced greee=n peppers,carrots, and other spices if you like and also chopped fried beef or chicken at this step)
  7. Pour the mixed eggs on the pan and fry it for 3- 6 minutes according the level you want your fried eggs
  8. Lay your friedd eggs on a chapati
  9. Add on tomato slices and cabbages and season with some more salt and pepper if you like
  10. Lay on another chapati on top and roll all of and now ready to serve
