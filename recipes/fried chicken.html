Fried Chicken




Ingredients

  • Chicken: 2kg of mixed chicken pieces
  • Marinade: 1 to 2 cups of buttermilk mixed with hot sauce or salt
  • Flour Coating: 3 cups all-purpose flour, ½ cup cornstarch (for extra crunch), paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper
  • Frying Oil: Vegetsble Oil

Steps

  1. Marinate the Chicken
    • Place the chicken pieces in a bowl or resealable bag with buttermilk and your choice of hot sauce or seasonings.
    • Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or anywhere from 2 to 24 hours to tenderize the meat.
  2. Prepare the Dredge
    • In a large bowl or shallow pan, combine the all-purpose flour, cornstarch, salt, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper.
    • For extra crispy crags, mix a spoonful of the wet buttermilk marinade directly into the dry flour mixture to form tiny rough flakes.
  3. Coat the Chicken
    • Remove each piece of chicken from the buttermilk and let any excess drip off.
    • Press the chicken firmly into the seasoned flour mixture, turn it over, and squeeze the flour onto the surface to help the crust bond.
    • For maximum crunch, dip it back into the buttermilk briefly and dredge a second time. Let the coated pieces rest on a wire rack for 5 to 10 minutes so the crust sets.
  4. Fry the Chicken
    • Pour about 3 inches of vegetable or peanut oil into a heavy-bottomed pot, Dutch oven, or cast-iron skillet.
    • Heat the oil to 340°F to 350°F.
    • Carefully lower the chicken into the hot oil in small batches so you do not overcrowd the pan (overcrowding drops the temperature and makes the crust greasy).
    • Fry dark meat (thighs and drumsticks) for about 12 to 15 minutes, and white meat (breasts and wings) for about 8 to 12 minutes, turning occasionally.
    • The chicken is done when the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) at the thickest part and the crust is deep golden brown.
  5. Drain and Serve
    • Transfer the finished chicken to a wire rack set over a baking sheet (avoid placing them flat on paper towels or plates, which traps steam and makes the bottom soggy).
    • Sprinkle immediately with a pinch of flaky salt and serve hot.
